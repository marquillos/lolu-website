Accessibility statement

Our commitment to accessibility

LoLu Accessibility Group wants everyone to be able to use this website, including people who use screen readers, keyboards, magnification, captions or other assistive technology. We hold our own site to the standard we review for our clients.

Standard

We aim to conform to the Web Content Accessibility Guidelines (WCAG) 2.1, Level AA.

How we test this site

  • Automated testing of every page with the axe-core engine
  • Keyboard-only navigation
  • Screen readers: NVDA on Windows and VoiceOver on macOS and iOS
  • Zoom to 400% and color contrast checks

Known limitations

We are not aware of any barriers on this site. If you find one, we want to know.

Feedback

If you have trouble using any part of this site, or need information in another format, contact us and we'll respond within 2 business days.
