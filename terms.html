Last updated October 5, 2026

These terms apply when you order a report from LoLu Ventures, LLC, doing business as LoLu Accessibility Group (“LoLu,” “we”). By placing an order you agree to them.

1. What we provide

We test a software product you identify against WCAG 2.1 Level A and AA, and write an Accessibility Conformance Report (ACR) on the VPAT 2.5 template. The standard edition covers up to 5 user flows you choose. The International edition also maps findings to EN 301 549. The yearly re-test updates an earlier report. We deliver the report as Word and PDF files.

2. What the report is, and is not

An ACR describes how your product conformed to WCAG at the time we tested it. It is a dated snapshot. It is not a legal opinion, a certification, or a guarantee that your product meets any law. We do not call any product “compliant.” Speak to a lawyer for legal advice.

3. What we need from you

  • A working test account for the product, created for this purpose.
  • No real customer data in that account.
  • Your confirmation that you have the right to let us test the product.
  • Prompt answers to our questions about the flows you chose.

Prices are the fixed prices shown on our site when you order. You pay in advance by card through Stripe. Our 5-business-day turnaround begins when we receive a working test account. Delays caused by an account that does not work, or by missing information, move the delivery date.
